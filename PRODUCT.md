# CareMap AI

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform
web

## Stack
Existing Next.js App Router project. TypeScript strict mode, Tailwind CSS, Lucide, Motion, Zod, server-side OpenAI SDK. Deployable to Vercel.

## Users
Patients, families, and people facing language barriers who need to understand medical instructions already supplied by healthcare professionals.

## Product Purpose
Turn a pasted visit summary into plain-language explanations, organized documented follow-ups, clarification questions, and an understanding check.

## Positioning
Each extracted action links to a verified exact quotation in the original document. Missing details are made visible instead of guessed.

## Operating Context
A solo-developer healthcare hackathon with a three-minute live demonstration. Fictional sample data must work without API credentials. Custom documents require a configured AI provider and explicit consent.

## Capabilities and Constraints
English, Uzbek, and Russian. Plain-text input, source inspection, temporary personal checklist, quiz, copy questions, print, reset. No accounts, database, analytics, document persistence, uploads, diagnosis, prescribing, or appointment booking. All model requests occur on the server. Source quotations stay in their original language.

## Brand Commitments
CareMap AI. Your health instructions, finally clear. Premium, accessible healthcare interface with Geist typography, a light #F7FAF9 canvas, #0D2732 text, #087F72 teal, and restrained amber uncertainty states. The detailed user brief fixes the design direction and delegates remaining decisions.

## Evidence on Hand
User-provided complete master prompt. Clearly fictional visit on October 8, 2026, follow-up October 15 at 10:30 AM, medication-list preparation, and unclear lab scheduling. No clinical validation, certifications, customer statistics, or endorsements.

## Product Principles
Source before interpretation. Clarity without clinical inference. Honest demo and live states. Privacy through minimal retention. Useful uncertainty.

## Accessibility & Inclusion
Three languages, keyboard navigation, semantic forms, visible focus, reduced-motion support, responsive layouts from 320px upward, readable source text, and accessible source drawers.
